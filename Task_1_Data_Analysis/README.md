# Task 1: Data Analysis

## Overview

The first task focused on analysing Commonwealth Bank social media data to identify useful patterns and insights that could support business decision-making.

The analysis examined social media posts, customer sentiment, engagement, topics, customer intent, and posts that required a response.

The purpose was to understand what customers were discussing, how they were responding to Commonwealth Bank content, and which areas could require further attention.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives were to:

- Explore the social media dataset.
- Identify the main topics discussed by customers.
- Analyse customer sentiment.
- Examine social media engagement.
- Identify customer intentions and concerns.
- Identify posts that may require a response.
- Determine how the findings could support business decisions.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contained 60 Commonwealth Bank social media records.

The dataset included information relating to:

- Post content
- Topics
- Sentiment
- Likes
- Replies
- Reposts
- Views
- Link clicks
- Total engagement
- Engagement rate
- Customer intent
- Response requirements

---

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Topic Analysis

The frequency of different topics was examined to understand which subjects appeared most frequently in the dataset.

The most frequently occurring topics included:

- Digital Banking: 13 posts
- Fraud & Scams: 11 posts
- Financial Wellbeing: 9 posts

This showed that digital banking, fraud prevention, and financial wellbeing were important areas of discussion.

---

### 2. Sentiment Analysis

The sentiment of the social media posts was analysed to understand how customers were responding to Commonwealth Bank content.

The dataset contained:

- 26 positive posts
- 15 neutral posts
- 11 negative posts
- 8 mixed posts

The results showed that positive sentiment was the most common category, while negative and mixed sentiment could be useful for identifying customer concerns and areas requiring attention.

---

### 3. Engagement Analysis

Engagement metrics were analysed to identify posts that attracted the most attention.

The analysis considered metrics such as:

- Likes
- Replies
- Reposts
- Views
- Link clicks
- Total engagement
- Engagement rate

The post with the highest total engagement was post SM048, which focused on protecting customers from "safe account" scams.

It recorded 12,165 total engagements.

Fraud and scam-related content also recorded high levels of engagement overall, suggesting that security-related topics generated significant customer interest.

---

### 4. Customer Intent Analysis

Customer intent was examined to understand the reasons behind interactions with Commonwealth Bank social media content.

Examples of customer intent included:

- Educational content
- Feature feedback
- Praise
- Complaints
- Questions
- Product updates
- Campaign-related interactions
- Scam reports

Understanding customer intent can help the organisation determine what customers are looking for and how different types of interactions should be handled.

---

### 5. Customer Response Analysis

The dataset included a field indicating whether a social media post required a response.

A total of 11 out of the 60 posts were identified as requiring a response.

This information could help customer service teams prioritise social media interactions and respond to customers more efficiently.

---

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several important patterns:

1. Digital Banking was the most frequently occurring topic.
2. Fraud and Scams generated particularly high engagement.
3. Positive sentiment was the most common sentiment category.
4. Security-related content attracted strong customer engagement.
5. Customer interactions included questions, complaints, feedback, praise, and scam reports.
6. Some posts required a direct response from the organisation.
7. Views and total engagement showed a strong relationship within the dataset.

---

## Business Applications

The findings could support several business areas.

### Customer Service

Customer intent and response indicators could help identify customers who require assistance.

### Fraud Prevention

High engagement with fraud and scam-related content could help the organisation understand customer interest in security education.

### Marketing

Engagement data could help identify the types of content that attract customer attention.

### Product Development

Customer feedback about digital banking and other services could help identify potential areas for improvement.

### Financial Wellbeing

Discussions around financial wellbeing could help the organisation understand customer interests and develop relevant educational content.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrated how social media data can be used to understand customer interests, sentiment, engagement, and concerns.

The findings show that social media data can provide useful information beyond traditional customer records and can support customer service, marketing, fraud prevention, product development, and strategic decision-making.

The analysis also provides a foundation for the next stages of the project, including data anonymisation, proposing data analysis approaches, and designing a structured database.
